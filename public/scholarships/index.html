BHCFL provides scholarship opportunities and partnerships to help learners access training, curriculum, and special events. Below are current scholarship offerings and ways to apply.

Jamilia Ahmed Memorial Scholarship

The Jamilia Ahmed Memorial Scholarship awards $250 to two qualifying students who are currently homeschooled and submit an essay about their educational goals.

Deadline: April 1 — award distributed at the BHCFL Graduation Awards Ceremony in May.
